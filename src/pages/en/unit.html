Conversions that trip people up

  • KB vs KiB — KB is base-1000, KiB is base-1024. The gap between your OS and the drive label comes from this (a 1 TB drive showing ~931 GiB)
  • Temperature — not expressible as a pure multiplier (it has an offset), so it cannot share a coefficient model with other units
  • US vs imperial gallon — this tool uses the US gallon (3.785 L). The imperial gallon is 4.546 L, about 20% larger
  • "month" and "year" — constant conversions of 30 / 365 days. Leap years and variable month lengths are not considered
