    How it works

    Randomness comes from crypto.getRandomValues (the OS cryptographic CSPRNG). Uniformity has been verified with a chi-squared test, and rejection sampling is used to eliminate modulo bias. There is deliberately no fallback to a weaker RNG — if a cryptographic source is unavailable, this tool throws instead of silently producing weak passwords.

    Generated passwords exist only in memory. They are not transmitted, stored or logged, and disappear when you close the page.
