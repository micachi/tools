Classic date-arithmetic bugs

  • Time zones — dividing a local-time millisecond difference by 86400000 shifts by a day across a DST boundary. Only avoided by normalising to UTC
  • Non-existent dates — if you don't reject 30 February, it silently rolls over and produces a wrong answer
  • "One month later" — one month after 31 January is 28 (or 29) February. Without a fixed rule, implementations disagree
  • Public holidays — this tool's business-day count excludes weekends only. Add a holiday table if you need real-world accuracy

The calendar picker renders according to your OS/browser locale, but the value is always YYYY-MM-DD, so results never depend on locale.
