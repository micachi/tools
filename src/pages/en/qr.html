Choosing an error-correction level

QR codes are designed to survive damage; higher levels tolerate more damage but store less data. As a rule of thumb: L for on-screen display, M for printing, Q/H for outdoor use or paper that bends. If you overlay a logo in the centre, use Q at minimum.
